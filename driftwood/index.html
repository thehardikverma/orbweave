The Challenge

Peak-Season Waste Was Eroding the Mission

8 Remote Lodges, 8 Separate Systems

Each lodge ran its own generators, water tanks and kitchen orders, with no shared view across the group.

22% Food Wasted

Kitchens over-ordered for peak weeks and composted the rest.

Only 8% Rainwater Reused

Rain tanks overflowed while diesel pumps refilled pools from the mains.

Our Solution

A Resource Agent Designed for Circular Hospitality

Orbweave deployed a resource agent with demand forecasting—balancing energy, water, and food across every lodge, quietly and continuously.

Demand Forecasting

Bookings, weather and local events feed a model that predicts meals, showers and laundry with 7-day accuracy.

Solar & Battery Scheduling

Shifts laundry, heating and pumps into sunny hours to maximise solar self-use based on the forecast.

Smart Kitchen Orders

Suggested orders like "Cut fish by 20%—two groups left early?" woven into the chef's morning routine.

PMS Integration

Smooth connection with existing Property Management Systems for real-time availability and occupancy forecasts.

Guest Nudges

Gentle prompts for towel reuse and shower timers plus post-stay impact reports guests love to share.

Ongoing Refinements

Continuous tuning and optimisation based on meter data and performance analytics.
